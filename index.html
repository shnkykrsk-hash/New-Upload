Not getting reach? Check these first

Ideas, titles and tags give you a starting point. Before judging the result, look at:

  • The first 3 seconds. Does the video give a viewer a reason to stay?
  • Thumbnail and title. Do they make someone curious enough to click?
  • Sample size. Try the approach on 3 to 4 uploads before deciding. One video is not a pattern.
  • YouTube Studio → Analytics. Check impressions click-through rate and average view duration.
  • Early traction. Did your existing audience watch or share it in the first few hours?
  • Tags. They help YouTube understand your topic, but title, thumbnail and watch time matter more.

These are content suggestions based on your channel's own data, not a guarantee of views, subscribers or earnings. Growth still depends on your content, consistency and audience.
